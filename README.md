# FreshTrack: Kitchen Inventory & Expiry Food Tracker

## Problem Statement
A lot of people throw away good food at home because they forget it is sitting in the back of their fridge. Groceries are expensive, but when people get busy, items pass their expiration dates unnoticed and end up in the trash. This wastes money and creates unnecessary food waste. People need an easy way to keep track of what food they have and when it is going to go bad. FreshTrack is a project determined to solve these issues to mitigate the cause of food waste by helping families track their fridge inventory, keep tabs on expiration dates, and cook meals using ingredients before they spoil.

## Project Objectives
1. Lets users quickly add and organize at least 15 foods with their expiration dates.
2. Show a list of daily food that will expire in the next 3 days so users know what to eat first.
3. Suggest simple meals users can cook using the food that is about to spoil.

## Planned Features 
- **Food Tracker:** Easily add, edit, or remove items in your kitchen with their expiration dates.
- **Color Warnings:** Color alerts food status whether it is still fresh and still can be eaten or it is spoiled and inedible. Green color represents that the food is still fresh and safe to eat, yellow color indicates that the food is about to expire soon (within 3 days), and red means it is expired or it is past its expiry date.
- **Meal Ideas:** Simple recipe suggestions based on the ingredients that need to be used right away.

## Planned Inputs and Outputs
### Inputs:
- Food name
- Category (Fruits, Veggies, Dairy, Meat, Condiments/Sauces, Eggs)
- Expiration Date
- Quantity

### Outputs:
- An organized list of all kitchen items
- Color-coded alerts
- Recipe ideas for food that is expiring soon
